# Glossary

**Quest giver**: A bear or bee NPC that hands out quests. Each one can be switched on or off in the macro.

**Quest**: A titled list of objectives from one quest giver, handed in when every objective is complete.

**Objective**: One row of a quest, such as gathering pollen in a field, defeating a mob, or feeding bees.

**Manual objective**: An objective the macro cannot do (or may not do, because its item toggle is off). The macro skips it, tells the user, and hands the quest in once the user has finished it.

**Fixed-line giver**: A quest giver with a set list of quests that ends. After the last quest is handed in, the macro switches that giver off.

**Locked giver**: A quest giver whose required number of bees is greater than the hive's bee count in config. The macro skips it even when its switch is on; the switch's note says how many bees it needs.
