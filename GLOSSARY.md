# Fuzzy Macro quests

Terms for quest requirements and the work needed to complete them.

## Language

**Quest giver**:
The character who offers a quest and accepts it when its requirements are complete.
_Avoid_: Quest, when referring to the character rather than the assignment.

**Quest**:
A named assignment from a quest giver, containing one or more objectives.

**Objective**:
One requirement within a quest, such as collecting pollen in a field or catching bloom petals.
_Avoid_: Quest part, subquest.

**Gather session**:
A period of gathering in a field that ends for completion, conversion, or an interruption. One quest objective may require multiple gather sessions.

**Quest giver priority**:
A scheduling policy that chooses ready quest work in the user's quest-giver order.

**Optimize quest completion**:
A scheduling policy that shares compatible work in the current field, then favors fields that advance more unfinished objectives. Quest-giver order breaks ties.

**Preferred quest field**:
A user's chosen field for a giver's objectives that do not require a specific field. The preference must satisfy the objective's color requirements.
